# Mobile craft

> The craft of phone apps. Read after `index.md`, which says how.

## The job
Phone apps, used in short visits and often with one hand: each screen has to be obvious at a glance,
and the whole has to feel at home on the phone it runs on.

## Positioning questions
Answer each in one line, from the brief, before the first frame.

- What is the one job of each screen? Two jobs make two screens.
- One hand or two: where is the phone when this gets used?
- Which platform's habits does it inherit: iOS, Android, or both, and where do they differ?

## What great looks like
- The main action in thumb reach: in the lower half of the frame, not in a top corner.
- The platform's navigation and gestures, respected: back, swipe and sheets behave as they do in the
  rest of the phone, and nothing redefines them.
- Type sized for a phone at arm's length: seen at real size, the smallest text on the frame still
  reads.
- States drawn: empty, loading, error and offline each have a frame, with the words they would show.
- Motion that explains: each caption says where a thing came from or went, and none plays as
  decoration.
- A real device frame with its safe areas: the notch, the status bar and the home indicator are
  drawn, and nothing that matters sits under them.

## Refuse
- A website squeezed into a phone: a top nav and a long scroll of sections.
- A hamburger hiding the main paths: what people do every day stays in view.
- A stack of same-sized cards: every item weighs the same, so nothing leads.
- Targets under 44 px: a thumb is not a cursor.
- A tab bar with more than five entries: past five, choose what leads.

## Deliverables
- The key flow, screen by screen, at the phone widths of `design.widths`.
- A sheet and a tab bar, where they apply.
- All of it in light and dark.

## Optional skills
- `mobile-native`: its symptom table and first fixes, for what a phone really does: tap feedback,
  the true viewport height, no zoom on an input.
- `apple-design`: response on press, 1:1 tracking and interruptible gestures, and a sheet that
  leaves by the path it came in.
- `emil-design-eng`: the animation decision framework, which tells whether a motion explains
  anything.
- `minimalist-ui`: type contrast, and colour kept for meaning on a surface too small for ornament.
- `redesign-existing-projects`: the audit's interactivity and states lists, to find the frame nobody
  drew.
- `frontend-design`: the traits generated design clusters around, and its self-critique.
- `ui-ux-pro-max`: the touch and navigation rows of its priority table.
- `imagegen-frontend-mobile`: its list of what mobile concepts collapse into, read as a checklist
  for the flow.
- `impeccable`: its craft floor and detector, as `index.md` says.
